[CLIENT]: Hi, I'm interested in getting hair extensions but I don't know anything about the different kinds. Can you walk me through what you offer?
[RECEPTIONIST]: Absolutely. We do tape-ins, hand-tied wefts, and we occasionally do fusion or i-tips if it's a special case. Most of our clients land on tape-ins because they're the most beginner-friendly and the easiest to maintain.
[CLIENT]: What's the difference between tape-ins and hand-tied?
[RECEPTIONIST]: Tape-ins are thin panels of hair sandwiched onto your natural hair with a medical-grade adhesive strip. They lay very flat and are great if you mainly want length or some thickness. Hand-tied wefts are sewn onto rows of small beads, which gives more volume and lasts a little longer between moves.
[CLIENT]: How long do they last?
[RECEPTIONIST]: Tape-ins need to be moved up every six to eight weeks as your hair grows. The hair itself, if cared for, lasts six to twelve months before you replace it.
[CLIENT]: What does maintenance look like at home?
[RECEPTIONIST]: Sulfate-free shampoo, no conditioner at the roots, brush with a loop brush twice a day, and braid loosely before bed. We send everyone home with a starter kit, the brush plus a small Moroccanoil and a sulfate-free wash.
[CLIENT]: What's the cost?
[RECEPTIONIST]: For a first install of tape-ins, you're looking at a consultation first, which is free. The install itself, with hair, ranges from eight hundred to fourteen hundred depending on how much we use. Each move-up after is about two-fifty.
