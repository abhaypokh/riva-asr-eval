Olaplex is a bond-builder, it works on a chemical level to repair the disulfide bonds in your hair that get broken during chemical processes like coloring or bleaching. Most of our stylists recommend it for anyone doing highlights, bleaching, or going more than two shades lighter or darker.
[CLIENT]: What does it actually feel like? Does it change how the appointment goes?
[RECEPTIONIST]: It's added as Number One into the color mixture itself, then Number Two is applied after the color is rinsed and sits for ten minutes. So your appointment ends up about fifteen minutes longer. The result is your hair feels noticeably stronger and shinier after.
[CLIENT]: And the cost?
[RECEPTIONIST]: As an add-on it's thirty-five dollars on top of any color service.
